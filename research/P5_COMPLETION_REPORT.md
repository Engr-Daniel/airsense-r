## Clean test results

Metrics average seeds within station, then weight stations equally. MAE and RMSE
are in micrograms per cubic metre of PM2.5.

| Family | MAE | RMSE | Mean station R² |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| XGBoost | 9.710861 | 18.920934 | 0.946611 |
| Persistence | 10.279643 | 19.654933 | 0.942216 |
| MLP | 10.810206 | 18.938473 | 0.946263 |
| GRU | 15.957414 | 37.165979 | 0.688383 |

The frozen validation-selected family remains **persistence**. XGBoost had the
lowest validation MAE, but persistence was within the predefined 1.0 near-tie
threshold and won the simplicity tiebreak. Final-test performance did not change
the selection decision.

Clean-test selection regret is 0.568782, with a paired 95% interval of
[0.339985, 0.763172]. XGBoost's advantage over persistence is statistically resolved
under this bootstrap, but remains within the frozen practical near-tie threshold.
These clean results do not establish ranking reliability under sensor degradation
or site shift. Those questions remain for P6/P7 and the later statistical analysis.
Intervals are conditional on the fitted seed set and this single-city dataset.
