## Main P6 finding

The validation-selected persistence model remained within the frozen
1.0 microgram/m³ practical near-tie threshold in all 15 full-period conditions
under each policy. It was numerically best in five conditions per policy.
Maximum full-period selection regret was 0.568606 under primary imputation and
0.551976 under no-forward-fill. The largest upper endpoint of any full-period
95% selection-regret interval was 0.763129. These are per-condition intervals,
not simultaneous coverage across the entire experiment matrix.

This supports the practical reliability of this particular clean-validation
selection decision under the predefined stresses. It does not show that every
model's ranking stayed fixed or that persistence is generally best. Historical
PM2.5 was excluded from the primary synthetic corruptions, so persistence's
unchanged predictions within each policy are a designed property of the study.
The findings do not cover failure of that target-history channel, physical sensor
faults, inter-city transfer or embedded deployment. P7 site shift remains untested.
